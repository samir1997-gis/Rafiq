1. Who we are

Rafiq (rafiq-arabic.com) is an app for learning Arabic, run by a business called Rafiq, based in Leicester, United Kingdom. We are the “controller” of your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can reach us about anything in this policy at support@rafiq-arabic.com.

We don’t ask for, and don’t want, sensitive information such as your religious beliefs or health. Rafiq teaches Arabic that many Muslims use for prayer, but using it doesn’t record anything about your faith.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide Rafiq to you (contract): your account, lessons, reviews, progress across devices, your plan and payments.
  • To email you about your account (contract): confirming your email, resetting your password, your welcome email, reminders before your free week ends, and messages about your plan. These aren’t marketing.
  • To help you and fix problems (legitimate interests): answering your messages and reports, and keeping Rafiq secure and working.
  • To keep records the law requires (legal obligation): for example, payment records for tax.

We don’t send marketing emails. If we ever want to, we’ll ask first, and you’ll be able to say no or change your mind at any time.

4. Smart answer checks

When you type an answer in your own words (a sentence, a reply in a conversation), Rafiq may check it on its meaning rather than letter by letter. To do this, the text you typed and the question are sent through our checking service (run on Cloudflare) to TypeSafe, which judges whether the answer is right. Your name and email are not sent, only the words of the question and your answer. We use the result to mark your answer and nothing else.

6. Storage on your device

Rafiq doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and doesn’t use analytics that follow you around the web. We count visits to our pages with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Your browser does store a few things for Rafiq, all needed for it to work:

  • keeping you signed in (in a web browser, signing you out after 24 hours without use, for safety on shared devices; the app on your home screen keeps you signed in)
  • your settings, such as the voice, sounds, keyboard and theme
  • a copy of your progress, so lessons still work when your signal drops
  • where you first came from (for example a link in an Instagram post), which is saved with your account if you sign up, so we know which posts help people find Rafiq

Clearing your browser’s site data removes these; your progress is kept safely in your account.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and learning: until you delete your account. Deleting it (Settings → Delete my account) removes your account, progress and settings straight away.
  • Payment records: Stripe keeps records of payments, and we keep invoices, for 6 years, as UK tax law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • Messages and reports: for as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Backups: our database provider keeps short-term backups, which are overwritten within days.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • correct anything that’s wrong (you can change your name in Settings)
  • delete your data, which you can do yourself in Settings
  • take your data with you in a common format
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it

To use any of these, email support@rafiq-arabic.com. We’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Children

Rafiq is for people aged 13 and over. Children under 13 can use Rafiq only with a parent or guardian’s permission: the parent or guardian should set up the account, or agree to it, and is responsible for any subscription. If you’re under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you subscribe. If you think a child under 13 has an account without a parent’s permission, email us and we’ll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects you in a big way, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before it happens.

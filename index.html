Good to know

🔍 What's a smart check?

Rafiq judges something you wrote on its meaning (a sentence, a reply, a spoken answer) rather than matching it letter by letter. When you use one, what you typed is sent to our checking service and on to TypeSafe, which does the judging. Nothing else about you is sent.

👩‍🏫 Who checks the Arabic?

The Arabic, its vowel marks and the meanings are written carefully and checked by a teacher. Rafiq teaches language, though: for questions about how to pray, ask a scholar. The audio is a clear, natural voice, good for listening and rhythm. For fine pronunciation, nothing beats a teacher.

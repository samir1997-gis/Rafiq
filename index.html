How a day works

Open the app and press Continue. Each step takes about five to ten minutes.

Meet — new words with audio, transliteration and a picture, then a quick check.
Listen — a short conversation, line by line, read aloud.
Understand — one grammar idea at a time, with examples.
Practise — build, fix and say sentences; answers are checked on meaning, so other correct wordings count.
Speak — have the conversation yourself, in your own words, and get feedback on each reply.

Everything you learn comes back for review just before you'd forget it. Can't read Arabic yet? Start with the reading starter. Already know some? A three-minute check puts you in the right place.

Good to know

A smart check is when Rafiq judges something you wrote on its meaning — a sentence, a reply, a spoken answer — rather than matching it letter by letter. When you use one, what you typed is sent to our checking service and on to TypeSafe, which does the judging. Nothing else about you is sent.

A study aid, not an authority. Vowel marks, model answers and grammar notes are carefully written but worth checking with a teacher, especially case endings. Built-in speech is good for rhythm and listening, not a pronunciation model.

## Opening takbir · تَكْبِيرَةُ الإِحْرامِ

_Said with raised hands to begin the prayer; also said at every movement._

- [ ] **اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ**
  Allah is the Greatest.

  | Arabic | Meaning | Root |
  |---|---|---|
  | اللَّهُ | Allah | أ-ل-ه |
  | أَكْبَرُ | is the Greatest | ك-ب-ر |

## Opening supplication · دُعاءُ الاسْتِفْتاحِ

_Said quietly after the opening takbir, before Al-Fatiha._

> **For the teacher:** Several authentic openings exist (e.g. اللَّهُمَّ باعِدْ بَيْنِي وَبَيْنَ خَطاياي...). This is the short one most commonly taught; the teacher chooses.

- [ ] **سُبْحانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعالى جَدُّكَ وَلا إِلٰهَ غَيْرُكَ**
  Glory be to You, O Allah, and with Your praise. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no god other than You.

  | Arabic | Meaning | Root |
  |---|---|---|
  | سُبْحانَكَ | Glory be to You | س-ب-ح |
  | اللَّهُمَّ | O Allah | أ-ل-ه |
  | وَبِحَمْدِكَ | and with Your praise | ح-م-د |
  | وَتَبارَكَ | and blessed is | ب-ر-ك |
  | اسْمُكَ | Your name | س-م-و |
  | وَتَعالى | and exalted is | ع-ل-و |
  | جَدُّكَ | Your majesty | ج-د-د |
  | وَلا | and there is no | — |
  | إِلٰهَ | god | أ-ل-ه |
  | غَيْرُكَ | other than You | غ-ي-ر |

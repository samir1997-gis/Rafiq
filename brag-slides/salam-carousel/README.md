## Title

These 5 Arabic words share one secret 🤫

## Description

Salaam, Islam, Muslim, saleem, salaama: all five grow from the same three letters, س ل م (s-l-m), the root of peace and safety. Spot the root and one word becomes a whole family.

And you say it at the end of every prayer: as-salāmu ʿalaykum 🤲

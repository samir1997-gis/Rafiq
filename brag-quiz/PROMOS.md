## How to film (all three)

- **Phone, portrait, screen recording.** Light mode, so the app matches the cream cards. Do Not Disturb on, so nothing pops up.
- **Keep what matters in the top 60% of the screen.** A portrait recording fills the panel from the top. The lower part is cropped,
  and the captions sit over it. (A landscape recording fits inside the panel instead.)
- **Slow, one-finger movement.** Scroll slowly and stop on what you want people to read. Anything you want read needs 2 seconds still.
- **Use an account in its free week, or on Complete.** Your salah is a Complete feature (the free week gives you everything).
  A brand-new account opens on *Most-said words 1–5*, which is the easiest thing to film.
- **Sign in before you press record.** Don't film the sign-in.
- **No Quran recitation, ever.** The Listen step of an Al-Fātihah part plays a licensed recitation. Do that step off camera with the
  phone on silent, or trim it. The earlier video plan kept recitation out of marketing too.
- **Record a little long**, then trim to the lengths below. If a recording is shorter than its slot, the last frame is held.

When you have a recording, save it as `brag-quiz/screens/<id>.mp4` and rebuild (see the end).

## p1: "I built a tool" (recording slot 3.8s to 23.0s, 19.2s)

Film it as **two takes joined into one file** (the app takes too many taps to get through in one slot).

**Take A, 4.7s (3.8–8.5): the website.**
1. Open **rafiq-arabic.com** in the phone's browser. Hold the home page for 2s.
2. Scroll slowly down about one screen.

**Take B, 14.5s (8.5–23.0): the word cards.** Sound **on** for this one (the app says the word when you tap it).
1. Home → **Your salah** → **Start: Most-said words 1–5**. The first screen is **Word by word**.
2. Pause on the first card for about 3s: **سُبْحانَ**, "Glory be to", "You say this 36 times in a four-rakah prayer", and its **root**.
3. Tap the Arabic word to hear it. Wait for the voice to finish.
4. Scroll slowly down through **رَبِّيَ**, **اللَّهُ**, **الْأَعْلى**. (The caption "Every word of your salah — explained." lands at 15.5s, over the lower part.)

*If you want **السَّلامُ** as in your script* (meaning "Peace", root س ل م), use the **Tashahhud** part. It's there once your account has reached it.

**Captions (built):** 15.5s "Every word of your salah — explained." · 20.3s "Free for a week. No card needed."

## p2: "Why I made Rafiq" (recording slot 4.0s to 22.0s, 18s)

2. 10.0–17.0: **Your salah**: the map of the prayer's parts ("The prayer, in order"). Scroll slowly.
3. 17.0–22.0: back to **Home**, held still on the **Continue** button.

## p3: "Al-Fātihah, word by word" (recording slot 3.2s to 18.6s, 15.4s)

One take, **phone on silent**. Your account needs to have reached **Al-Fātihah (1)** (it opens after the Most-said words and the
opening parts), or have done it already; a finished part can be opened again.
1. Open **Al-Fātihah (1)**. Do the Listen step off camera, silent. Start recording on **Word by word**.
2. 3.2–10.5: the first line's words (**بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**), scrolling slowly down the cards. Then tap **Next line**
   for **الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ**.
3. 10.5–16.2: pause about 2s each on two or three words (**الرَّحْمَٰنِ**, **رَبِّ**, **الْعَالَمِينَ**): meaning and root are on each
   card. Tapping a Quran word makes no sound, which is right.
4. 16.2–18.6: hold, or keep scrolling, under the caption.

**Caption (built):** 16.2s "Then do the same for your whole salah."  The outro adds "Free for a week · no card needed".

## The sound

The fountain from `brag-output-v6` under everything, with `tap.mp3` as each caption arrives and `correct.mp3` on the key line, on the
same frame. No music. The quiz videos' bed (`data-volume 0.12`) measures about -55 dB, which is silent (#217), so here it's lifted
6 dB and played at 0.8. Measured on the renders: the bed alone is about -34 dB, a tap and the chime land about 5 dB above it
for their first fraction of a second, and each whole video is about -28 LUFS (the quiz videos are -29 to -32). Soft, but there.
The recordings in `screens/` aren't committed (they're large and yours); the rendered placeholders are.

## Putting your recording in, and the next batch

```
cp my-recording.mp4 brag-quiz/screens/p1-i-built-a-tool.mp4
python3 brag-quiz/build_promo.py p1-i-built-a-tool
cd brag-quiz/out/p1-i-built-a-tool/composition && npx hyperframes render -o ../p1-i-built-a-tool.mp4
```

- `screen.audio` in `promos.json` sets how loud the recording's own sound is (0.9 for p1, 0 for the others).
- `screen.taps` (seconds into the video) puts a `tap.mp3` on each tap in your recording.
- `python3 brag-quiz/build_promo.py --check` measures every caption and hook line (inside the frame, clear of TikTok's buttons).
- A new promo is one more entry in `promos.json`: `kicker`, `hook`, `screen` (with `shots`), `captions`, `outro`.

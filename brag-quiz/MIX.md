- **The keyboard** is the standard Arabic layout.
- **Worth a glance from the teacher (general knowledge, written for the videos):**
  - **m03:** sugar, lemon and safari came into English through Arabic (safari via Swahili, from safar); tea came from Chinese, and Arabic شاي did too. Pizza is Italian.
  - **m02:** "zero" comes from ṣifr (via Italian).
  - **m05:**
    - "ر doesn't join the letter after it"
    - "ة only ever comes at the end"
  - **m10:**
    - ق is "a 'k' from deep in the throat"
    - ك is "a plain English 'k'"
  - **Translations shown:** طَويل is "tall" (as the app has it) against قَصِير "short".
- **m08:** the Day 1 blur is a joke about how it feels, not a claim about anyone's progress.

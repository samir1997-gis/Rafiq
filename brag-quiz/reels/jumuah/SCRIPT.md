# Jumuʿah: 3 things (talking to camera, about 40 s)

Plain edit: silences cut, clean sound, captions, the gold pops on stressed words. On screen from the first frame:
**"Don't let this Jumuʿah pass without these 3 things"** (so people who don't understand the Arabic still stay).

**Opener (Arabic, slowly, smiling)**
الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي بَلَّغَنا جُمُعَةً أُخْرى
*Al-ḥamdu lillāhi lladhī ballaghanā jumuʿatan ukhrā.*
"Praise be to Allah, who let us reach another Jumuʿah."

**Hook**
Don't let this Jumuʿah pass without these **three** things.

**1. Pray Jumuʿah**
Number one: pray Jumuʿah. The Prophet ﷺ said the five prayers, and one Jumuʿah to the next, **wipe out** what comes between them,
as long as the major sins are avoided.
*(Muslim 233)*

**2. Send salawat on the Prophet ﷺ**
Number two: send **salawat** on the Prophet ﷺ, a lot. He told us to send more of it on Friday, because it's **presented to him**.
*(Abu Dawud 1047)*

**3. Read Surah al-Kahf**
Number three: read **Surah al-Kahf**. Whoever reads it on Friday, a **light** shines for them from one Friday to the next.
*(al-Hakim and al-Bayhaqi; graded authentic by al-Albani, Sahih al-Jamiʿ 6470)*

**Ending**
Send this to someone to remind them. 🤍

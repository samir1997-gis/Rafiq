# Your Jumuʿah checklist, in order (talking to camera, about 55 s)

Plain edit: silences cut, clean sound, captions (the Arabic sentence on screen as you say it, then the English), the gold
pop on stressed words. On screen from the first frame: **"Your Jumuʿah checklist, in order"**.
Each step: say the Arabic slowly, pause, then the English. Keep each step to about 6 seconds.

**Opener**
الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي بَلَّغَنا جُمُعَةً أُخْرى
*Al-ḥamdu lillāhi lladhī ballaghanā jumuʿatan ukhrā.*
"Praise be to Allah, who let us reach another Jumuʿah. Here's your checklist, in order."

**1.** اِغْتَسِلْ *Ightasil.*
"Take a ghusl. The Prophet ﷺ strongly urged it on Friday." *(Bukhari 879)*

**2.** قُصَّ أَظافِرَكَ *Quṣṣa aẓāfirak.*
"Trim your nails. It's from the fitrah, and part of getting ready for Jumuʿah." *(fitrah: Muslim 257)*

**3.** اِلْبَسْ أَحْسَنَ ثِيابِكَ وَتَطَيَّبْ *Ilbas aḥsana thiyābika wa taṭayyab.*
"Wear your best clothes and put on perfume." *(Bukhari 883)*

**4.** بَكِّرْ إِلى الجُمُعَةِ *Bakkir ilā l-jumuʿa.*
"Go to Jumuʿah early. The earlier you go, the greater the reward, like giving a camel in charity." *(Bukhari 881)*

**5.** أَكْثِرْ مِنَ الصَّلاةِ عَلى النَّبِيِّ ﷺ *Akthir mina ṣ-ṣalāti ʿala n-nabiyy ﷺ.*
"Send lots of salawat on the Prophet ﷺ. It's presented to him." *(Abu Dawud 1047)*

**6.** اِقْرَأْ سُورَةَ الكَهْفِ *Iqraʾ sūrata l-kahf.*
"Read Surah al-Kahf: a light for you from one Friday to the next." *(al-Hakim, al-Bayhaqi; graded authentic by al-Albani)*

**7.** اُدْعُ فِي آخِرِ ساعَةٍ *Udʿu fī ākhiri sāʿa.*
"Make duʿa in the last hour before Maghrib. There's an hour on Friday when duʿa is answered." *(Bukhari 935; the last hour: Abu Dawud 1048)*

**Ending**
"Send this to someone to remind them." 🤍

**For the teacher before posting:**
- the Arabic of each step (vowels; the imperatives are to one man: اِغْتَسِلْ, قُصَّ, اِلْبَسْ, تَطَيَّبْ, بَكِّرْ, أَكْثِرْ, اِقْرَأْ, اُدْعُ);
- the wording of each benefit and its reference;
- nails: trimming is from the fitrah; the reports tying it to Friday specifically are weak, so it's framed as getting ready;
- ghusl: "strongly urged" (scholars differ on whether it's obligatory, Bukhari 879 says wājib);
- Jumuʿah prayer is obligatory for men, optional for women.

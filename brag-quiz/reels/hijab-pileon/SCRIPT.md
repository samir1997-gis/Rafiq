**[The pile-on … HIJAB! HIJAB! — dead cut. Walk in.]**

Hijab.
*(beat)* Everyone's got an opinion on it.
But here's something most people don't know: when the Qur'an tells women to cover… it uses a **different word**.
And the word *hijab*? It's in the Qur'an too, and it means something even bigger.

Ḥ-j-b 🎬 *ح ج ب*: to screen, to block from view.
- The wall between **Paradise and the Fire**? That's a hijab 🎬 *(7:46)*.
- When the **sun sets** and disappears? It goes behind the hijab 🎬 *(38:32)*.
- When Allah spoke to **Mūsā**? From behind a hijab 🎬 *(42:51)*.
- And the Prophet ﷺ said Allah's own hijab is **light**. If He lifted it, the glory of His Face would burn everything His sight reached 🎬 *(Muslim 179)*.

Now, before Allah says a single word to women about covering… He speaks to the **men**: "lower your gaze" 🎬 *(24:30)*.
The first hijab is on the eyes.

Then for women He says *khimār* 🎬 *(24:31)*. And ʿĀʾishah said the women didn't wait. Didn't debate. They tore their own cloaks and covered
themselves, straight away 🎬 *(Bukhārī 4758)*.

Quick test 🎬 *3-2-1*: there's one part of your face that Arabic calls a *ḥājib*, a little hijab. Which one?
3… 2… 1… your **eyebrow** 🎬 *an eyebrow over a closed eye*. Because it guards your eye.

So a hijab is never just a piece of cloth.
In the Qur'an, it's what stands between **something precious** and the world.

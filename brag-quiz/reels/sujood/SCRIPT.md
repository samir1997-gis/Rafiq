# Script: the words you say in sujood (about 40 s)

Part 2 after the rising-from-ruku reel (`../samia`). Kept to about 40 s: the 73 s ruku reel lost people in the middle
(13% watched); the 46 s re-cut is on trial. Edited with the reel template (`../template`).

Say each beat as its own take, with a short pause between beats. **Bold** = say it with punch (the edit zooms on those).

---

**[ANGLE A: head-on]**

**1. Hook** *(flicker; on screen: "LOWEST → MOST HIGH")*
When your head is at its **lowest**… you call Allah the **Most High**.

**2. The line** *(the words light up one by one over the top, speaker below)*
In sujood you say: *subhana rabbiyal a'la.*
*Subhana*: glory be to. *Rabbiya*: my Lord. *Al-A'la*: the **Most High**.

**3. The root** *(title behind the head: ع ل و "HIGH")*
*A'la* comes from **high**. Same root as the name **Ali**, and *ta'ala*, as in "Allahu ta'ala".

**[ANGLE B: camera on your left, turn to it]**

**4. Sitting between the two** *(title behind the head: "FORGIVE ME")*
Then you sit up, and you ask: *Rabbighfir li, warhamni.*
My Lord, **forgive** me, and have **mercy** on me.
*Ighfir*: same root as *astaghfirullah*. *Irhamni*: same root as *ar-Rahman*.

**[ANGLE A: back to head-on]**

**5. How often** *(the count runs up to 102)*
You say *subhana rabbiyal a'la* over **a hundred** times a day.

**6. Closest** *(slow push-in, no title)*
And the Prophet ﷺ said you're never **closer** to your Lord than in sujood.
*(pause)* Now you know what you're saying there.

**7. Ending (record both, we pick one)**
- **A, content post:** Which part of your salah should I do next? **Comment** it.
- **B, app post (about 1 in 5):** Rafiq teaches you every word of your salah. Free week, link in bio.

**Numbers** (the app's data: `salah-data.js`, counts from `salah.js` REPS `sujud: [24, 4]`):
- *Subhana rabbiyal a'la*: 3 times in each prostration, 2 prostrations a rakah = 6 a rakah. 17 rakahs a day → **102**.
- *Rabbighfir li*: once a rakah, sitting between the two prostrations → 17 a day.

**Check with the teacher before posting:**
- Beat 4 is said **sitting between** the two prostrations, not in sujood itself (the script says so).
- The longer wording, *rabbighfir li warhamni wahdini wajburni wa'afini warzuqni* (Abu Dawud 850, at-Tirmidhi 284;
  the wordings differ a little), against the app's short *rabbighfir li*. The script uses just *li warhamni*.
- Some add *wa bihamdih* ("and with His praise") after *al-a'la*; the schools differ on it, so the script leaves it out.
- Beat 6: "The closest a servant is to his Lord is when he is in prostration, so make much du'a" (Sahih Muslim 482).

## The script

The word **dajjāl** comes from three letters: **dāl, jīm, lām**. *Dajala*: to coat, to cover.

The Arabs used it for coating a sick camel in tar, so you couldn't see the mange underneath. And for gilding: painting
gold over something cheap, so it looks precious.

And *dajjāl* is the strong form, like *kadhdhāb*. So a dajjāl isn't just a liar. He's a **master liar**: someone who
covers the truth with something shiny, again and again.

So by its meaning, anyone who does that is a dajjāl. There could be dozens, even hundreds of them, living among us. The
Prophet ﷺ even said: *the Hour won't come until about thirty dajjāls appear, liars, each claiming to be a messenger of
Allah.*

But there's one who's the greatest of them all: **al-Masīḥ ad-Dajjāl**.

And his coating is the biggest lie there will ever be. The Prophet ﷺ said he comes with something like Paradise and
something like Fire, and what he calls Paradise **is** the Fire. He paints Hell gold.

Why *al-Masīḥ*? That's also ʿĪsā's title. Same word, opposite meaning. ʿĪsā is the true Masīḥ, and he's the one who will
end him.

But Allah didn't leave us without a clue. Between his eyes there's a word written: **kāfir**. And the Prophet ﷺ said
every believer will read it, whether they can read or not.

Think about that. You won't need to be able to read. You'll need **īmān**. The paint can't cover that word.

And you already ask for protection from him in every salah: *Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika… min sharri fitnati l-Masīḥi
d-Dajjāl.* And the Prophet ﷺ said: whoever memorises the first ten ayahs of Sūrat al-Kahf is protected from him.

So learn to see through the gold paint. And learn the words that protect you.

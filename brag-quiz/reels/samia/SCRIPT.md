# Script: the words you say rising from ruku (about 45 s)

Say each beat as its own take, with a short pause between beats. **Bold** = say it with punch (the edit zooms on those).
"·" = a slow count (the zoom steps once per word).

---

**[ANGLE A: head-on]**

**1. Hook**
Every time you stand up from ruku, you're having a **conversation**.
*(pause)* Here's what's being said.

**2. The first line**
As you rise, you say: *sami'a Allahu liman hamidah.*
*Sami'a*: **hears**. *Allahu*: Allah. *Liman*: the one who. *Hamidah*: **praises Him**.
Allah hears the one who praises Him.

**[ANGLE B: camera on your left, turn to it]**

**3. The answer**
And straight away, you **answer**: *Rabbana wa lakal hamd.*
Our Lord, and to You belongs **all praise**.

**4. How much of your prayer**
Those two lines are just **seven · words**.
But in a four-rakah prayer, **one in every fifteen** words you say is in these two lines.

**5. How often**
You say them **seventeen** times a day.
That's over **six thousand** times a year.

**[ANGLE A: back to head-on]**

**6. Praise runs through it**
And that word for praise, *hamd*, runs right through the prayer.
Even the name **Muhammad** means "the praised one".

**7. Ending (record both, we pick one)**
- **A, content post (TypeSafe: more respectful and clearer, 2.90 vs 2.56):** So next time you rise from ruku, you'll know exactly what you're saying. **Follow** for more of the words you say in salah.
- **B, app post (about 1 in 5):** Rafiq teaches you every word you say in salah. Free week, link in bio.

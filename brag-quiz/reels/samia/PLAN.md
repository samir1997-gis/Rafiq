with a frame of the 3-things clip standing in for the new footage).

Numbers come from the app's data (`salah-data.js`, with the counts per four-rakah prayer from `salah.js`):
- Each line is said once per rakah: 4 times in a four-rakah prayer, 17 times a day in the obligatory prayers (2+4+4+3+4), 6,205 times a year.
- The two lines are 7 words, 28 of the 425 words in a four-rakah prayer: about 1 in 15 (6.6%), not counting the surah you add.
- The root ح م د ("praise") comes up 21 times in one four-rakah prayer, counting the Prophet's name, Muhammad ("the praised one").

# Founder video (#393, for the landing page #386)

Two cuts from one sitting: a **short one for the landing page** (about 25 s, above the "Start" button) and a **longer one for Instagram** (about 50 s). Film the long one; the short one is cut from it.

Change any line that isn't true for you. Say it the way you'd say it to a friend, not read out.

## The long one (Instagram, about 50 s)

> **Every day, five times a day, I was saying words I didn't understand.**
>
> Subḥāna rabbiya l-ʿaẓīm. I'd said it thousands of times. And one day I realised I didn't know what it meant.
>
> It means "Glory be to my Lord, the Magnificent". And the next time I said it in rukūʿ, it felt completely different.
>
> I'd tried the big apps. They taught me how to order coffee. Not the words I say to Allah every day.
>
> So I built Rafiq. It starts with the words you already know by heart, and teaches you what they mean, one word at a time. A few minutes a day, and the words come back for review just before you'd forget them.
>
> If you've ever stood in salah wishing you knew what you were saying, start here. The first lesson's free. Link in bio.

## Filming

- Phone upright, at eye level, a window in front of you (not behind). A clip-on mic if you have one; a quiet room if not.
- Look at the lens, not the screen. Start talking straight away: the first line is the hook, with no "hi guys" before it.
- Do the first line three or four times on its own: it's the one that matters most.
- Leave a second of silence after each paragraph, so I can cut between them.
- Send the raw clip (or clips) as they are; I'll edit it with the approved reel template and captions, and cut the short version.

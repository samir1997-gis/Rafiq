## Where it comes from

- **f1:** `vocab-data.js` 1/2 (salam and its reply), 3/6 (kayfa ḥāluka, al-ḥamdu lillāh), 110/184 (shukran, ʿafwan).
- **f2:**
  - كِتاب (`vocab-data.js` 150) and مَكْتَبَة (279).
  - كَتَبَ from `toolkit-data.js` VERBS.
- **General knowledge, worth a glance from the teacher:**
  - The six letters that never join the next one: ا د ذ ر ز و.
  - "ك ت ب = writing".
  - "praise be to God (I'm well)" as the everyday sense of al-ḥamdu lillāh in reply to "how are you?".
- **Yusuf** is a made-up name for the chat.
